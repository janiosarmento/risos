People Love This Porsche Christmas Ad Because It Doesn't Look Like AI Was Involved

Gizmodo·Mike Pearl·agora·abrir original
Resumo IA llama-3.3-70b · 2,1s

O anúncio de Natal 2025 da Porsche ganhou destaque justamente por, supostamente, não ter usado inteligência artificial — o oposto da campanha do McDonald's, retirada do ar após reações negativas.

A peça, criada pela Parallel Studio, combina desenhos manuais e CGI numa estética 2D clássica. Nem a Porsche nem o estúdio confirmaram oficialmente a ausência de IA, mas nenhuma menção ao uso da tecnologia aparece em seus canais.

O anúncio inclui detalhes da história da marca e mudanças de estação, atraindo fãs de longa data.
A campanha do McDonald's feita com IA foi considerada uma das piores do ano e retirada do ar.
A percepção de "não-IA" se espalha nas redes mesmo sem confirmação do estúdio.

Conteúdo original

What if there were an ad on TV, and it wasn't made with AI?

For plenty of people on the internet, that's apparently an exciting and praiseworthy prospect. Here, for instance, is the 2025 Porsche Christmas ad.

I like this ad. It has a certain high-class elegance, but it's also as gentle and frictionless as an episode of Cocomelon. If you're considering being in the market for a Porsche, it might build some nice brand associations.

In the ad, the seasons change. A car gets driven around. There are little details about Porsche history and culture that reward multiple watches. Most importantly, it has a classic 2D animated feel that comes from a mix of hand-drawings and CGI, according to a Porsche Instagram post.

What's getting people excited, however, is the assumption that no AI was used to make it. After all, this year's McDonald's Christmas ad made with AI would rank high on any list of the worst ads of all time.

"Very good to see non ai stuff these days," the top comment on the version of the ad posted by Parallel Studio says.

Gizmodo reached out to Parallel Studio to confirm that the ad was really made without AI, and will update if we hear back.
